Your rules: the level rule pulls a quote while best-level imbalance is beyond the threshold. The jump rule pulls the quote about to be hit for 30 seconds when top-5 imbalance moves that far from its 30-minute average. The kill switch counts trades and book updates in 10-second bins and fires above 5 standard deviations of the previous six hours (it needs 30 minutes of history); it then pulls both quotes for 10 minutes and can close the position at the prices on the book, paying the spread.

Assumptions: quotes join the back of the queue at the best bid and ask and never create new price levels. We are filled only by size beyond what was queued ahead of us, so the fills are conservative. Our orders do not change what other traders did, which holds only for sizes small next to the book. Inventory left at the end of each loaded window is marked at the mid, not sold.
